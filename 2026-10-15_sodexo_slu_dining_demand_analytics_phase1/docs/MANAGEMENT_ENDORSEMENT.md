# Management Review, Approval and Commitment to Implement

**Project:** Sodexo SLU Dining: Inventory & Demand Analytics, Phase I
**Analyst:** Olawale Ajibola Ashaolu, Data Analyst, Sodexo, Saint Louis University Dining Services
**Reviewed by:** Director of Operations – Retail, Sodexo, Saint Louis University Dining Services
**Status (October 2026):** Approved. Recommendations accepted for implementation.

## What was reviewed
I presented the complete Phase I package to the Director of Operations – Retail:

- the Data Analysis Report: findings, 12 charts, and 8 recommendations
- the Key Indices Reference: 29 indices with definitions, formulas, and SLU values
- the Phase I continuation blueprint: 7 stages over 26 weeks
- the underlying datasets parsed from the Sodexo Inventory Summary History Report (INRHTSUM), plus the Grand Hall Day Parts, procurement-template, and catalog evidence

## Outcome of the review
1. **Approval to use the data and publish the work.** The Director approved the use of the project files, datasets, charts, and reports, including publication in this repository under the Sodexo and Saint Louis University names.
2. **Endorsement of the findings and recommendations.** The Director reviewed the eight recommendations in [RECOMMENDATIONS.md](RECOMMENDATIONS.md) and endorsed them. He added his own recommendations and guidance on implementation priorities.
3. **Commitment to implement.** The Director committed to implementing the recommendations in SLU Dining operations. These include:
   - PAR levels for the 409 Grand Hall procurement lines
   - weekly physical counts at the units with high stale-count rates
   - correction of the Panera outlier
   - new waste and receiving logs
   - the Starbucks inventory review
   - catalog clean-up
   - support for the demand forecasting model

## Why this matters for the project
Management sign-off moves Phase I from analysis to institutional adoption. In the terms of the NIW proposed endeavor:

| Endeavor step | Evidence from this review |
|---|---|
| **Develop** | The analytics pipeline, datasets, and indices were reviewed as working products |
| **Validate** | The findings were checked by the operations leader responsible for the units involved |
| **Deploy** | Management committed to putting the recommendations into practice across the units |
| **Publicly disseminate** | Approval was given to publish the work openly in this repository |
