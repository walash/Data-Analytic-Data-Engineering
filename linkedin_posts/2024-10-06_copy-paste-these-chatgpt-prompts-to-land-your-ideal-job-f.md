## Content

Copy & paste these ChatGPT prompts to land your ideal job (FAST) 👇

Stand out to top employers, ace interviews & accelerate your career!

Use these 7 powerful prompts:


✍️ Prompt 1: Power-Packed LinkedIn Invite

Craft a compelling LinkedIn connection request for [Target Name] at [Dream Company]. Highlight a shared interest and suggest a valuable exchange. Limit to 300 characters. Provide 3 versions: formal, casual, and industry-specific, with tips on when to use each. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Their recent activity: [Recent Post/Article]. 


✍️ Prompt 2: Story-Driven Cover Letter

Create an engaging cover letter for [Job Title] using the "Hero's Journey" writing structure. Align my career goal with [Company]'s mission. Present as a structured outline with key storytelling elements highlighted. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Job Description].


✍️ Prompt 3: 24-Hour Resume Makeover

Transform my resume for the [Title] role at [Company]. Identify 5 quick-win improvements and 3 strategic long-term changes. Provide a prioritized action plan with time estimates for each change. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description]. 


✍️ Prompt 4: Rapid Skill Acquisition Plan

Create a 30-day plan to develop the top 3 skills for [Target Role]. Include daily 20-minute practice sessions, weekly milestones, and resources. Present as a calendar with daily tasks and progress tracking metrics. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


✍️ Prompt 5: Experience Reframing Workshop

Reposition my experience as [Current Title] for a [Target Role]. Identify 5 transferable skills and create compelling stories for each. Deliver as a worksheet with prompts for crafting each story. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


✍️ Prompt 6: 60-Second Video Pitch Script

Write a script for a 60-second video application for [Job Title] at [Company]. Include a hook, key qualifications, and a unique value proposition. Provide the script with visual and audio cues for effective delivery. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


✍️ Prompt 7: Customized Interview Simulator

Design a tailored interview simulation for [Specific Role] at [Company]. Include 5 common and 5 curve-ball questions based on the company's culture and recent news. Present as an interactive Q&A with real-time feedback options. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].
