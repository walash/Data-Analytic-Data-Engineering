## Content

I still remember that week.

Laptop open on one side… job portal on the other.
Apply. Apply. Apply.

100+ applications.
0 calls.
Not even a “let’s talk.”

At first, I blamed myself.

“Maybe my skills aren’t enough.”
“Maybe my projects aren’t impressive.”
“Maybe the market is just bad.”

So I did what most people do.
I rewrote my resume again.

Changed words. Changed fonts. Changed headings.
Polished every line.

Still nothing.

Then someone told me one sentence that changed everything:

“Your resume isn’t bad. It’s just not getting read.”

That’s when it clicked.

Recruiters don’t read resumes.
They scan them.
And ATS doesn’t care how hard you worked… it cares how clearly your resume is structured.

So I made a few small changes (nothing dramatic).

Same skills. Same projects. Same experience.
Different presentation.

And within the next couple of weeks… calls started coming in.

Here’s what I changed (and what I’d recommend if you’re stuck right now):

✅ 1) I made it skimmable in 6 seconds
• Clear sections (Skills, Experience, Projects, Education)
• No paragraphs
• Only the strongest points on the first half-page

✅ 2) I rewrote bullets to show impact, not responsibilities
Instead of “Worked on dashboards”
→ “Built dashboards that reduced reporting time by 10+ hours/week”

Instead of “Did ETL”
→ “Built an ETL pipeline that improved data quality and reduced failures by X%”

✅ 3) I matched keywords to the job description
Not copying. Just aligning language so ATS can understand you.

✅ 4) I removed ATS blockers
• Fancy icons in header
• Over-designed templates
• Tables that break parsing
• Too many fonts / weird spacing

Most resumes don’t get rejected because the person isn’t capable…
They get rejected because the resume doesn’t communicate fast enough.
