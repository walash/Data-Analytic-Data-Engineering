♐️Behind every slick data dashboard, smart recommendation engine, and automated insight lies an intrepid explorer charting territories unknown—the data engineer.

🔘DATA ENGINEERING is a mindset shift that primarily focuses on - designing, building, and maintaining data pipelines and infrastructure to collect, store, process, analyze, and visualize data emphasising on development, construction, and maintenance of the systems and infrastructure required.

- Designing and implementing data architectures
- Building and maintaining data pipelines
- Developing infrastructure
- Data modeling
- Performance Optimization
- Data quality and governance
- Supporting analytics/BI teams
- Collaboration and Communication
