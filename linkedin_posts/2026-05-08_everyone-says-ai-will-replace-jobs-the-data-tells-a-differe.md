## Content

Everyone says AI will replace jobs. The data tells a different story.

I analyzed 10,000+ job postings from 2016–2026. Here's what the future of work ACTUALLY looks like:

1️⃣ AI support roles are growing FASTER than pure AI engineering.
AI trainers, prompt engineers, and AI ethicists saw 136% growth — while core AI engineering grew 34%. The people who teach and govern AI are winning.

2️⃣ Green energy trades are the #1 fastest-growing category.
Wind turbine techs (+50%) and solar installers (+42%) are outpacing every tech role. Skilled trades + tech = the new six-figure career path.

3️⃣ Cybersecurity demand is outpacing software engineering.
35% projected growth vs. 17% for traditional software dev. Every company needs defenders — not just builders.

4️⃣ Human skills now command a salary premium.
Workers combining creativity, empathy, and AI literacy earn 56% more than peers without these skills. The "soft skills" era is over — they're now PREMIUM skills.

5️⃣ Healthcare + AI hybrid roles are exploding.
Nurse practitioners (+40%), AI-powered diagnostics specialists, and health data scientists are the fastest-growing high-paying careers. $130K+ median salaries.
