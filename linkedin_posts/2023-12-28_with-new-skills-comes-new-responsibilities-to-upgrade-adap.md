🎉With new skills comes new responsibilities to upgrade, adapt and prove yourself.

👩‍💻SQL has been the goto skill in demand for engineers, developers, analysts, scientists to understand data more prominently and excel in analysing it from scratch.

♐️Heres's an interesting SQL Zero to Hero guide curated by Manu Agrawal and Tutort Academy to share the basic concepts without any hustle.

Few of the major concepts to prepare and work on includes - 
-> Aliases - To rename columns or outcomes
-> Group By - Group identical data arranging into different segments
-> Order By - Sort data in ascending or descending order
-> Joins - To join two or more tables to get common attributes
-> Functions - To perform various operations on data
-> Where - Filter the records based on the specific condition
-> Subqueries - Nested queries used to perform complex operations

If you are looking to prepare for SQL interviews,
