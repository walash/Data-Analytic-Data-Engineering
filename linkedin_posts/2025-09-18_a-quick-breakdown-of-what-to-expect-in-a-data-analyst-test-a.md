## Content

A quick breakdown of what to expect in a data analyst test at Amazon.

The focus is on these pillars:
Advanced SQL : GROUP BY, HAVING, and query optimization.

Python for Data : Manipulation with Pandas and handling large datasets.

Business Intelligence : Power BI questions, focusing on measures vs. columns.

Business Acumen : Metrics, dashboard design, and A/B testing.

Culture : Questions based on the company's leadership principles.
