Machine Learning Engineer Checklist Part 1

This checklist ensures that a Machine Learning Engineer is well-equipped with the necessary skills, tools, and processes to build, deploy, and maintain ML models efficiently.
1. Fundamentals of Machine Learning & AI
✅ Understand Supervised, Unsupervised, and Reinforcement Learning
 ✅ Knowledge of Classification, Regression, Clustering, and Anomaly Detection
 ✅ Experience with Feature Engineering, Data Preprocessing, and Feature Selection
 ✅ Understand Bias-Variance Tradeoff, Overfitting, and Underfitting
2. Mathematics & Statistics
✅ Proficiency in Linear Algebra (vectors, matrices, eigenvalues)
 ✅ Understanding of Probability & Statistics (Bayesian inference, distributions)
 ✅ Mastery of Calculus & Optimization (Gradient Descent, Lagrange Multipliers)
 ✅ Understanding of Information Theory (Entropy, KL Divergence)
3. Programming & Software Engineering
✅ Strong proficiency in Python (or R, Java, C++)
 ✅ Knowledge of OOP, Data Structures, and Algorithms
 ✅ Experience with Version Control (Git, GitHub, GitLab)
 ✅ Ability to write Modular, Scalable, and Efficient Code
 ✅ Proficiency in Unit Testing & Debugging
4. Machine Learning Frameworks & Libraries
✅ Hands-on experience with TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn
 ✅ Familiarity with Hugging Face Transformers for NLP
 ✅ Experience with OpenCV for Computer Vision
5. Data Handling & Engineering
✅ Experience with Pandas, NumPy, Dask for data manipulation
 ✅ Ability to handle Large Datasets (Spark, Hadoop, SQL, BigQuery)
 ✅ Proficiency in Data Cleaning & Imputation Techniques
 ✅ Understanding of Data Warehousing & ETL Pipelines
6. Model Training, Tuning, and Evaluation
✅ Implementation of Hyperparameter Tuning (Grid Search, Random Search, Bayesian Optimization)
 ✅ Cross-validation techniques like K-Fold CV, Stratified Sampling
 ✅ Experience with Evaluation Metrics:
Regression: RMSE, MAE, R²
Classification: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC-AUC
 ✅ Model Interpretability using SHAP, LIME
7. Deep Learning & Neural Networks
✅ Understanding of CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs, Transformers, GANs
 ✅ Experience with Transfer Learning & Pretrained Models
 ✅ Proficiency in Neural Network Optimization (Dropout, Batch Normalization, Adam, SGD)
 ✅ Ability to fine-tune LLMs (BERT, GPT, T5, Llama, Falcon)
