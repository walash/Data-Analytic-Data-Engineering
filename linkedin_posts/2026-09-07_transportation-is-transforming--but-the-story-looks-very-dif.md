## Content

Transportation is transforming — but the story looks very different depending on where you stand.

Global container port throughput hit 920 million TEUs in 2024 (+6.9% YoY), US international air passengers surged to 256.9 million (+8%), and African airline demand grew 8.5%. On the surface, it's a boom. But dig into the data and the divergence is striking.

🔋 EV Adoption: A Tale of Two Paths
Global EV car sales crossed 17 million in 2024 — over 20% of new-car sales. The US reached 1.6 million units (>10% share). Africa? Just ~11,000 EV cars. Less than 1%.

But here's what the headline misses: Africa's electric revolution is happening on two wheels. Electric motorcycles and three-wheelers — the backbone of last-mile delivery and ride-hailing across the continent — grew 38% YoY in 2025. That's a leapfrog moment, not a lag.

🚢 Logistics Costs: The Hidden Tax on Growth
Africa handles only 5% of global container traffic, yet logistics costs run 50–75% above the global average. For landlocked economies, transport costs can eat up 60% of trade value. Compare that to the US, where $36.8B/year in Bipartisan Infrastructure Law funding is actively closing supply chain gaps.

Africa's infrastructure investment shortfall? An estimated $150 billion annually. That gap isn't just an economic problem — it's a data problem. Better freight visibility, predictive logistics, and smarter port analytics could unlock billions in efficiency gains.

📊 The data opportunity here is massive for analysts who can bridge global benchmarks with regional realities.
