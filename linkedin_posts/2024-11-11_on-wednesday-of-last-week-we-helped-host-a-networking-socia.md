## Content

On Wednesday of last week, we helped host a networking social with the African Professional Network on campus! For this event, we collaborated with the African Chamber of Commerce St. Louis, and attendees met with chamber board members, African professionals, and business owners within the community. They also learned about professional opportunities and ACCSTL’s mission.

Our International Employer Career Outreach Specialist, Precious Jackson, worked closely with Waheed Shittu and Olawale Ashaolu from the African Professional Network and with Dr. Linda Ogbah Faas and Zellipah Githui with ACCSTL to make this event happen. She also received support from our Associate Provost, Jeffery L. Jackson, Ph.D., and our Assistant Director of International Career Services, Yolanda D. Mercer.
