📝Life is a rush into the unknown....🔥

What's the meaning of this life...🤔

Each person’s life has it's own unique, context specific meaning.
 
Being grateful for the little things we have is more important in #life than being miserable about the many things we don’t have.

Time flies, everything fades while the golden moments in the reels of life rush past us and all we are left with are the memories.

When we don’t know the values of our lives,we live with the priorities of others, instead of our own.

Life is the meaning you give it...
It's up to you what meaning you give to your life, your actions, your #career, your #business, your #future, your present, your friends, etc...

At some point, the angels come to visit us and before we realize, we have already gone to be with them. 👼😇

Our destiny is not something to be discovered. It's already with us.

The purpose of life is to live it before it's gone....
