I was job hunting wrong for months.
I was only using LinkedIn.

Then I realized — LinkedIn is where everyone looks. Which means it's also where you compete with everyone.

So I built my own list of platforms that most people completely ignore.
Here are 18 websites to find high-paying remote jobs (many pay in USD):

`REMOTE JOB BOARDS`

01. Remotive
Curated, fully remote tech jobs trusted by top global companies
→ remotive.com
02. We Work Remotely
Largest remote work community — 4.5M+ monthly visitors
→ weworkremotely.com
03. RemoteOK
Aggregates remote jobs across industries with salary filters
→ remoteok.com
04. FlexJobs
Remote + flexible jobs globally, includes career coaching
→ flexjobs.com
05. Remote Woman
Remote job board built specifically for women
→ remotewoman.com

→ theaijobboard.com
07. ML Jobs
Dedicated ML engineering and research roles
→ mljobs.com
08. AIJobs.net
Curated AI/ML/Data Science positions globally
→ aijobs.net


`STARTUP FOCUSED`

09. Wellfound (AngelList)
130K+ tech jobs from 27K startups
→ wellfound.com
10. Work at a Startup
Y Combinator's official job board for YC-backed companies
→ workatastartup.com
11. Cutshort
AI-powered job matching for tech roles
→ cutshort.io


`DEV SPECIFIC`

12. JS Remotely
JavaScript-only roles — React, Vue, Node.js, Angular
→ jsremotely.com
13. Toptal
Exclusive network for top 3% of freelancers globally
→ toptal.com


`FREELANCE & TALENT`

14. Hubstaff Talent
Free platform connecting businesses with remote freelancers
→ hubstafftalent.net

→ workwave.com

16. iimjobs

→ iimjobs.com

→ indeed.com

18. Eztrackr
Track all your applications in one place — no more spreadsheet chaos
→ eztrackr.com
