The global transportation sector is at a crossroads — and the data tells a fascinating story of divergence. 🚢️🚛

Here's what stood out from the latest 2024-2025 numbers:

📦 Global container port throughput surged 6.9% in 2024, yet freight costs doubled mid-year as Suez Canal disruptions rerouted 70% of tonnage around Africa's Cape of Good Hope.

✈️ Africa's air passenger traffic grew 13.2% — more than double the US rate — signaling explosive demand for connectivity on a continent building its aviation future in real time.

💸 Africa's logistics costs consume up to 30% of shipment value, nearly 4× the US average of ~8%. That gap isn't just a challenge — it's a $68-108B annual infrastructure investment opportunity.

⚡ EV adoption tells a tale of three speeds: Global at 20% of new car sales, US at 10%, and Africa at ~2% — yet African cities like Nairobi and Dakar are already deploying electric bus fleets and e-motorcycle networks for last-mile delivery.

The US is optimizing a mature system. Africa is building one from scratch. Both are racing toward the same destination: smarter, greener, more resilient transport networks.

For data professionals, this sector is a goldmine — freight analytics, route optimization, EV infrastructure modeling, and supply chain resilience are among the fastest-growing analytics use cases globally.
