2. Explain the difference between batch processing and streaming processing.
3. What is Delta Lake, and how is it different from a traditional data lake?
4. Explain the concept of partitions in Spark.

8. What is the difference between broadcast join and shuffle join in Spark SQL?
9. How does checkpointing work in Structured Streaming, and why is it important?
10. What are Z-ordering and Optimize in Delta Lake, and when should you use them?
11. Explain schema evolution and enforcement in Delta Lake.
12. What are some strategies to handle late-arriving data in streaming pipelines?
