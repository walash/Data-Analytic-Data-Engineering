I spent 100+ hours reviewing every free AI agent course so you don't have to.

Here's the only guide you'll need in 2025:

🔥 For absolute beginners: 
• Elements of AI 
- https://lnkd.in/gwnrgXSn 
The only starting point that doesn't assume you know what a neural network is.

🔥 For developers new to AI: 
• Microsoft's AI Agents for Beginners 
- https://lnkd.in/gr3_2VaQ 
Skip the theory, dive straight into working code. 
Their GitHub repo is gold.

🔥 For serious practitioners: 
• Hugging Face's Agent Course 
- https://lnkd.in/gdneBcUs 
The closest thing to a university-level education you'll get for free.

🔥 The framework specialists: 
• DeepLearning.AI (crewAI) 
- https://lnkd.in/ggmWRSRG 

• LangChain Academy 
-https://lnkd.in/gkWeUP83
Learn directly from the creators (worth every minute).

🔥 Business implementation: 
• Salesforce AI Agent Course 
- https://lnkd.in/ga7AwMib 
Finally, someone explaining AI agents without requiring a CS degree.

🔥 The hidden gem nobody talks about: 
• Stanford CS25 
- https://lnkd.in/gk-BTzJc 
World-class education completely free. 

Start with Lecture 8 on agents.
The brutal truth about learning AI agents: 
• 90% of success is just doing the exercises 
• Most people quit after the first tutorial 
• The frameworks change monthly 
• The fundamentals haven't changed in years

My recommended learning path:
Start with Elements of AI (2 weeks)
Move to Microsoft's course (3-4 weeks)
Pick ONE framework and build something real
Stop course-hopping. Pick one path and complete it.
