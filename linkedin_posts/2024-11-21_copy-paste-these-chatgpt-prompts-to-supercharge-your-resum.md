Copy & paste these ChatGPT prompts to supercharge your Resume/CV 👇

3X your response rate. Secure interviews faster. Land your dream job!

Use these 10 powerful ChatGPT prompts:


📈 Prompt 1: Resume Executive Summary

Write a dynamic 3-sentence executive summary for [Target Role]. Include my unique value proposition, key achievement with metrics, and career vision. Keep it under 50 words. Present as three versions: achievement-focused, leadership-focused, and innovation-focused. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description]. 


📈 Prompt 2: Achievement Story Generator

Transform 5 work responsibilities into powerful achievement statements for [Industry]. Use specific metrics, percentages, and dollar values. Create before/after comparisons with impact metrics highlighted. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


📈 Prompt 3: Power Skills Showcase

Identify and present my top 7 skills for [Position] using the pyramid method - core skills at base, specialized at top. Demonstrate each with a brief success story. Present as a visual hierarchy with proof points. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


📈 Prompt 4: Resume Section Prioritizer

Reorganize my resume sections for maximum impact in [Target Role]. Create a visual flow that guides recruiters through my most relevant qualifications first. Show section arrangement with attention-heat-map. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


📈 Prompt 5: Expertise Highlighter

Develop compelling proof points for my expertise in [Field]. Include 3 major projects, 2 innovations, and 1 industry contribution. Present as achievement snapshots with measurable outcomes. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


📈 Prompt 6: Technology Skills 

Create an impactful presentation of my technical skills for [Tech Role]. Group by proficiency level and relevance to target role. Generate a skills matrix with proficiency indicators. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


📈 Prompt 7: Career Progression Emphasizer

Highlight my career growth from [Starting Role] to [Current Position]. Show increasing responsibility, leadership, and impact. Create a growth timeline with key milestones. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].


📈 Prompt 8: Project Impact Showcase

Turn 3 key projects into compelling resume highlights for [Position]. Focus on challenges overcome, innovations introduced, and results achieved. Present as mini case studies with outcomes. My resume: [Paste Resume]. Job description: [Paste Description].
