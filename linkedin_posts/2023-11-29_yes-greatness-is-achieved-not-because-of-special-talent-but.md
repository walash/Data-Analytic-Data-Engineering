## Content

Yes, greatness is achieved not because of special talent but rather due to dedication, perseverance and commitment. Show me a person with tenacity and persistence, I will show you laurels of successes. Other dogs gave up, but one dog stayed focused, persevered, and won the “meal”. 

Watch how the dog improved its tactics, as it learned from previous failures. Indeed, failure is not an opposite of success, but part of success. Without those failures, it would not have figured out how to ace the last lap.
