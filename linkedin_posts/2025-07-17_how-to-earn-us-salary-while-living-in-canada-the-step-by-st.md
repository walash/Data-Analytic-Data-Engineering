How to Earn US Salary While Living in Canada (The Step-by-Step Guide)

Want to land a US job? Here are the 3 proven pathways for Canadians right now (for remote work OR relocation).

Let's break down the strategy.

1. Find Companies That Hire Globally for Remote Roles.

‣ The secret is that the talent pool is now global. Smart US companies know this and are actively hiring in Canada to find the best people. The key is to look where they post.

‣ Go to Niche Job Boards: Websites like We Work Remotely, Remote.co, and Arc.dev are built for remote-first companies, so the location barrier is much lower.

‣ Use a Smarter LinkedIn Search: Filter by "United States" as the location, but add keywords like "Canada eligible" or "North America remote" to your search. This will surface the US companies looking for you.

2. Use the Independent Contractor Route.

This is the most direct way to work remotely for a US company without them needing a Canadian office. It's simpler than you think.

‣ First, you get a job offer from the US company that agrees to this structure.
‣ You then operate as a self-employed individual (a sole proprietor) in Canada.
‣ You invoice the company for your services, usually in USD.
‣ You are responsible for managing and paying your own Canadian taxes (income tax, CPP, and HST/GST).

3. Secure a TN Visa to Relocate.

For those who do want to move to the US, this is the most straightforward path for many Canadian professionals in fields like tech, finance, healthcare, and science.

‣ The TN Visa is part of the USMCA trade agreement.
‣ It is not a lottery.
‣ If you have a job offer in one of the ~60 eligible professions and hold the required qualifications, the application process is significantly simpler than other US work visas.

Whether you want to work from your living room or move stateside, there is a path for you. The key is knowing which one to pursue.
