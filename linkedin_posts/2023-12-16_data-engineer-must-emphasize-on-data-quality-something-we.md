## Content

✅Data Engineer must emphasize on Data Quality, something we all know, but we aren't closely working on it to solve real time problems that may impact data solutions.

👉I was intrigued when I came across Robert Hawker's book - "Practical Data Quality." While data engineers focus on building and maintaining pipelines, this book offers numerous benefits for data engineers -

📍Equips with a comprehensive understanding of data quality concepts. 
Helps in effectively communicating the importance of data quality within their organizations which fosters a data-driven culture that values accurate and reliable data.

📍Focus on data profiling and assessment techniques or identifying data quality issues, such as outlier detection, data completeness analysis, and data consistency checks to uncover hidden data anomalies and establish proactive measures. 

📍It offers insights into data deduplication, data standardization, and data enrichment methods, enabling data engineers to enhance the quality and usefulness of their data assets.

📍Depicts practical strategies and techniques that data engineers can implement to improve data quality leading to more reliable analyses and better-informed decision-making.

➡️It is well structured into three sections PPT - people, process, and technology emphasizing the significance of people, focusing on the organizational processes with technological capabilities through new tooling and extensions to enhance governance.
