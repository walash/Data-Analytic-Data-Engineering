✋🏿Gmail has 4.2 billion users.

🙅🏿‍♂️But only 0.1% know how to use it effectively.

🥵Here are 10 Gmail tips you may not know:

🤯(The number 7 is really mind-blowing):
________________

✋🏿(1) Undo the email you just sent.

➡️ Here's how to do it:

→ Settings icon (top right).
→ Settings>General.
→ Turn on 'Undo Send'.
→ Choose 30 seconds.
_______

✋🏿(2) Get rid of promotional emails.

→ Type 'unsubscribe' in the Gmail search bar.
→ Then select all the emails with an unsubscribe label.
→ Finally, just hit the delete button to clear them all.
_______

✋🏿(3) Send a confidential email.

You can make your email confidential by disabling copying, printing, and forwarding features.

To activate this, click on the padlock symbol when sending a new email.
_______

✋🏿(4) Check your Gmail offline

Follow these simple steps to enable Gmail's offline mode:

→ Log into your Gmail account
→ Click on the gear icon or 'Quick Settings'
→ View all settings and click on them
→ Select 'Offline'
_______

✋🏿(5) Use Gmail with Keyboard Shortcuts

Shift + I: Mark as read.

✔ Cmd or Ctrl + Enter: Send an email.
✔ Cmd or Ctrl + K to insert a link.
C to compose.
✔ / to search in your emails.
D to draft in a new tab.
_______

✋🏿(6) Send Your Email Later

Want to make it seem like you're at your desk at 10 PM?

You can do this:

→ Click the down arrow next to 'send'.
→ Choose 'Schedule send'.
→ Select the date and time you want.
_______

✋🏿(7) Use Labels

Create custom labels for different types of emails, like 'Work', 'Personal', or 'Travel'.

→ Just click on the 'Labels' button in the toolbar and select the label
you want to use.
_______

✋🏿(8) Creating Templates

→ Select Compose.
→ Enter the text you want to use for the template in the email field.
→ Select the three-dot menu, then Templates.
→ Choose Save draft as template.
_______

✋🏿(9) Mute Conversations
Reduce email overload by muting less important email threads.
→ Open the email conversation you want to mute.
→ Click on the three-dot menu.
→ Select 'Mute'.
→ The conversation will move out of your inbox and won't return until you unmute it.
_______

✋🏿(10) Archive Important Emails (Don’t Delete Them!)
