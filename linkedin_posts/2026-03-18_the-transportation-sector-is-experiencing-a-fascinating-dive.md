## Content

The transportation sector is experiencing a fascinating divergence across global markets—and the data tells a compelling story.

In the United States, transportation remains a cornerstone of economic activity, contributing 6.3% to GDP in 2024. While freight volumes show modest fluctuations in early 2026, the sector demonstrates resilience with truckload spot rates up 2% and LTL rates climbing 5.2%.

But here's where it gets interesting: Africa is emerging as a high-growth frontier. The continent's freight logistics market is projected to surge from $184.55M in 2025 to over $300M by 2034—a 62% expansion. Even more striking? Africa's aviation sector is booming with an 18.6% YoY increase in international seat capacity (March 2026) and 273 million projected air passengers in 2025.

Globally, the autonomous vehicle market is set to hit $65 billion by 2027, signaling a technological transformation that will reshape logistics, urban mobility, and supply chains across all regions.

The takeaway for data analysts and strategists: transportation isn't just about moving goods and people—it's a leading indicator of economic vitality, infrastructure investment, and technological adoption. The US maintains its leadership position, Africa presents explosive growth opportunities, and global innovation is accelerating faster than ever.
