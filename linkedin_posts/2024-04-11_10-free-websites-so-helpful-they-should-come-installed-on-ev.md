## Content

10 free websites so helpful they should come installed on every browser:

1. 12ft Ladder

Is there a valuable article locked behind an annoying paywall?

Drop the URL into 12ft Ladder.

All sites have a non-paywall version they send to Google for SEO.

12ft finds this version and serves it to you for free. 

Now you’re in.

2. UnrollMe

Is your Inbox unusable with 60+ spam emails a day?

Use UnrollMe to bulk unsubscribe from useless emails.

Take back control of your inbox. 

3. Darebee

Want workout inspiration but not sure where to go?

Access 1800+ workouts via this database. 

Most of the workouts are body weight and require no equipment.

Never feel uninspired to workout again.

4. PDF24 Tools

PDFs are a nightmare to edit. 

With this tool: 

• Edit PDFs without paying Adobe massive fees
• Easily learn how to use its intuitive tools / features

5. Quillbot

Get inspiration on how to repurpose your content.

• Input text and hit Rephrase
• Get AI inspiration on how to rewrite
• AI checks for plagiarism and credits sources 

6. Remove Bg 

Remove image backgrounds automatically in 5 seconds.

• Upload your image 
• AI removes the background
• Seamlessly add in new backgrounds

7. Jitter Video 

Canva but for motion design. 

Animate your designs to bring them to life: 

• Easy, intuitive UX/UI
• Hundreds of pre-built templates 

8. Render Forest 

The all-in-one branding platform. 

Create high-quality: 

• Videos 
• Logos
• Graphics 
• Mockups 
• Websites 

9. Smart Mockups 

Create free mockups for any device or physical product. 

It's a web app that lets you generate high-quality mock-ups for your: 

• Presentations
• Social media campaigns 
• DTC product

10. Snapdrop 

Airdrop but not exclusively for Mac. 

Don't leave your Android and PC friends behind. 

• Easily transfer files across OS 
• High-speed download / uploads
