## Content

🛢️ The Oil & Gas data tells a fascinating story heading into 2026 — and it's one every data analyst should be watching.

Here's what the numbers reveal:

📊 Global oil production is forecast to hit 107M barrels/day in 2026, while consumption sits at 105.2M b/d. That's a growing surplus of ~1.9M b/d pushing inventories up and prices down — Brent crude projected at $70/b by Q4 2026 (EIA).

🇺🇸 US production growth is slowing to 3% YoY (13.6M b/d), as investors prioritize capital discipline over output expansion. Meanwhile, data center power demand is surging by 7.7 GW, reaching 48.3 GW — creating a massive pull on natural gas.

🌍 Africa is the breakout story. Upstream investment is forecast at $41 billion, with production hitting 11.4M b/d. Nigeria is targeting 2.5M b/d, Angola holds above 1M b/d, and Namibia's Venus and Mopane discoveries could reshape the continent's energy map.

💡 The real game-changer? Big Data in Oil & Gas is projected to reach $8.04B in 2026 (up from $6.98B in 2025) — a 15.1% CAGR driven by AI analytics, digital twins, and cloud migration. Companies using predictive maintenance and real-time production optimization are pulling ahead.

The convergence of oversupply, energy transition pressures, and AI-driven efficiency is creating unprecedented demand for data professionals who can translate complex energy datasets into strategic decisions.
