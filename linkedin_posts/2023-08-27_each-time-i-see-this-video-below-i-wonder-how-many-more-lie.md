## Content

Each time I see this video below, I wonder how many more lies we have been told, especially about Africa. As I grow and discover the beauty, excellence and values of our indigenous people, such as the belief in shared prosperity, the understanding of a more effective way of transmitting entrepreneurial skills from one generation to the next through apprenticeship, and the belief in the power and role of women in society, I marvel. This is because while contemporary society are still struggling with these issues, Africa has always had powerful women who were Queens, warriors and wealthy merchants. I sometimes wonder what Africa would've become without the negative interference of colonialism. 

I definitely see the value and richness in cultural integration, when countries come together for mutual benefit. But when it's self-centred and skewed to ensure the benefit of one to the detriment of the other, then its a bad thing.

I recently saw a video where the French people who were asked to leave Niger, were crushing vehicles in good condition,  to ensure they leave no benefits to the Nigeriens. I shuddered!!! 

Our world is definitely wounded and needs healing. May all those of goodwill find a way to influence people in their circle to do good. We can certainly do better. We must do better! 

There can never be peace when countries seek to exploit each other rather than collaborate for mutual benefit.
