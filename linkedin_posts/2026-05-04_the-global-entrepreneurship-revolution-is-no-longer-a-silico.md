## Content

The global entrepreneurship revolution is no longer a Silicon Valley story — it's a worldwide movement reshaping economies across 5 continents.

I analyzed startup ecosystem data across USA, China, India, Africa, and South America. Here's what the numbers reveal:

USA remains dominant with 1.56M startups, 853 unicorns, and $163B in VC funding (2025). But its ecosystem growth rate of 18.2% is the LOWEST among the top 50 countries.

China is surging at 45.9% growth, with Beijing climbing to #5 globally. Its 246 unicorns and deep-tech intensity in AI are reshaping the competitive landscape.

India now has 128 unicorns (3rd globally), with Bengaluru jumping 7 spots to #14. Fintech and SaaS dominate, and 6 new unicorns emerged in 2025 alone.

Africa's story is the most compelling — 40% YoY funding growth to $3B, with fintech capturing 35% of investment. Lagos, Nairobi, and Cape Town are emerging as serious innovation hubs despite infrastructure challenges.

South America saw $4.1B in VC funding, with fintech commanding 61% of all investment. Brazil and Mexico captured 78.5% of regional capital, and Colombia's growth rate exceeds 22%.

The surprising insight? Emerging markets (Africa at 40%, China at 46%) are growing 2-3x faster than the USA. AI captured 50% of global VC in 2025, and fintech leads in 4 of 5 regions.

By 2030, India and Africa are projected to see 4-5x growth in VC funding, fundamentally shifting the global startup power balance.
