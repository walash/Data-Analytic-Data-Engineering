Is cloud a mandatory skill for data engineers ❓

✅ Yes

☁️ Cloud whispers secrets of data, and in the hands of engineers, it becomes a symphony of insights that reshape the world.

🔰From big data being the most demanding technologies today the demand for cloud such as AWS, GCP or Azure is high, with changing times to have multi-skilled professionals.

✔️Talking about what a data engineer must be good at or try to upskill includes the following -

♐️Programming - Python, Scala, and Java could be any of the ones you could pick.
♐️Databases - SQL or NoSQL both are in demand, prefer being good at RDBMS, data querying is the most important skill you can’t omit this.
♐️BigData Tools - Spark, and Kafka are a few of the most in-demand skills to know, including knowledge of the Hadoop ecosystem.
♐️Data Storage - Data Lakes, warehouses and a lot more solutions must be explored.
♐️Cloud Computing: Cloud providers like AWS, GCP, and Azure are trending and shall continue to be with enormous services to build data pipelines.
♐️Automation-Scripting: Creating Automated solutions with tools or scripts play an important role for data engineers, to simplify tasks.
♐️BI Tools - Having Data Analytics and BI knowledge helps to implement data flow and gather insights.
♐️Communication and Presentation: Be good at explaining even before approaching the solution.
♐️Data Security, Governance, and Data Contracts are important to know even if you’re not completely involved in these activities.
♐️Problem-Solving and Analytical Skills: It's important to think out of the box and think in an innovative and cost-effective manner.
