## Content

Transportation is moving at two very different speeds — and the data tells a fascinating story. 🚛✈️

Africa's air passenger traffic surged 13.2% in 2024 — one of the fastest-growing aviation markets on the planet. Meanwhile, the US freight market contracted, with trucking volumes dipping from 11.41B to 11.27B tons. Two regions, two trajectories.

Here's what the numbers reveal:

 Logistics costs as % of GDP: ~8.75% in the US vs. ~14.6% in Africa vs. ~12.5% globally. The infrastructure gap is real  and it's a massive opportunity for investment.

 US EV adoption has plateaued at just 7.5% of new vehicle sales, while China crossed 50%. Policy uncertainty and charging infrastructure gaps are the culprits.

🛵 Africa's ride-hailing market hit $2.53B in 2025 and is projected to reach $3.25B by 2031 — driven by urbanization, mobile money, and a digitally-native youth population. Motorcycles dominate, not sedans.

🌊 Global cargo volumes are forecast to grow just 2.4% in 2026 — modest, but the Asia-Pacific corridor (44.7% market share) and a recovering Europe-Asia air freight route (+14.7%) are bright spots.

The transportation sector isn't just about moving goods — it's a proxy for economic health, infrastructure maturity, and technological adoption. Data analysts who understand these dynamics are increasingly valuable to logistics, mobility, and infrastructure firms.
