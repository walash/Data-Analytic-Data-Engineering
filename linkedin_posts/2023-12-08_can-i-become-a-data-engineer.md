Can I become a Data Engineer ?

➡️Asked a lot of data enthusiasts, software engineers, testers and even a lot more people outside this industry.

📍Here I’m sharing few important concepts to learn and explore topics that are important as a data engineer- Python and SQL, Big Data Hadoop, Spark and Kafka, Cloud Computing, System Design, Distributed systems, Data Processing(Batch/Streaming), data catalog, Data Governance and security. 

- Other than these based on my experience If you have 1-3 years of experience as a data engineer, you can expect following questions:

>Data modeling
>ETL processes
>Data storage and warehousing
>Data pipelines
>Programming languages and tools

- Having 3-6 years of experience as a data engineer, you can expect to be asked more intermediate-level interview questions that explore your deeper understanding of data engineering concepts and your experience working with more complex data systems:

>Data architecture
>Big Data
>Data streaming
>Cloud computing
>Data governance and compliance

- If you have 6+ years of experience as a data engineer, you can expect to be asked more advanced and specialized questions that delve into your deep understanding of data engineering concepts and your experience working with complex data systems:

>Advanced data modeling
>Performance tuning and optimization
>Data integration
>Data orchestration
>Data security and compliance
>Advanced cloud computing
