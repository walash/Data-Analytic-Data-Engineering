The global transportation sector is being reshaped — and the data tells a fascinating story of divergence and convergence. 🚛✈️🌍

Here's what the numbers reveal for 2024-2026:

🇺🇸 United States: Trucking still dominates at 72.7% of domestic freight, with cross-border trade hitting $1.6 trillion in 2024. But logistics costs remain elevated at 7.8% of GDP — a signal that efficiency gains are still needed even in the world's most mature freight market.

🌍 Africa: The continent is the fastest-growing frontier. Air passenger traffic surged 13.2% in 2024, and AfCFTA is projected to drive a 28% increase in intra-African freight demand by 2030. The catch? An $68–108B annual infrastructure investment gap that must be bridged.

🌐 Global: Last-mile delivery now accounts for 53% of total shipping costs — making it the single biggest cost driver in logistics. The global logistics market is on track to reach $14.39 trillion by 2029, fueled by e-commerce and supply chain digitalization.

What's striking is the EV convergence: the US saw 10% EV sales growth in 2024, while Africa's EV market is growing at a 10.26% CAGR through 2033. The electric transition isn't a wealthy-nation story anymore — it's global.

The real opportunity for data professionals? Transportation generates enormous datasets — route optimization, demand forecasting, emissions tracking — and the industry is hungry for analytical talent.
