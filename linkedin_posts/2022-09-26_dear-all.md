Dear All,

Proten International is currently recruiting a clearing and logistics manager for one her client in the chemical production and sales industry. 

Responsibilties

#clearing 

Obtain an accurate check list and check all documents to avoid errors, identify missing documents, and/or identify potential issues.

Develop documentation required for shipping/clearing of shipment and submit to all relevant agencies and maintain/file copies as required.

Liaising with an accredited clearing agent to expedite clearance of all shipments in Apapa, Tin can island or the airport (as the case may be)

Handling timely order execution &amp; documentation like Pre &amp; Post shipment documents.

Administering import procedures and documentation and effective liaison with Nigerian Customs / Logistics.

#logistics 

Managing the seamless movement of the consignments to the destination.

Collaborate with vendors and suppliers to ensure all operations (shipping, delivery, etc) meet quality and safety standards.

Coordination between his/her company and other companies to ensure the right goods or products are delivered in the right quantities, to the right customer, at the right time with necessary documentation.

Developing and designing strategies for the supply chain and coming up with methods to plan and control goods and information flow in a supply chain.

Requirements

A Bachelor’s degree in social science required.

Master’s degree is related field.

5-7 years cognate working experience in supply chain management
Proficiency in international documentation, shipping processes and bill of lading.

Excellent networking with customs brokers, forwarders, and international partners

Good operational knowledge of the customs and excise HS Code, explanatory notes, general port regulations, and government agencies.
