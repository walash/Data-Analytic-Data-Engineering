## Content

Last week was very special. It was an honor to celebrate African culture, heritage and connect members of our community to businesses and opportunities within the Saint Louis region.

It was great to honor Vitendo4africa ,Ghanaian Association of Greater Saint Louis, Africana Restaurant , Sodexo and the African Diaspora Federal Credit Union for their immense partnership with the African Professionals Network over the years. We also honored Joseph Dwumaah Owusu ,Waheed Shittu, MBA and Olawale Ashaolu  who are the founders of the African Professionals Network.

Special thanks to our guest speakers, Pari Sheth, Esq. ,Evelyn Kumeh , Annie Jacqueline Mbale, MBA , KODJO ANANI , Adaldino Sapi, BSc, AWS-CSA , Joan R. Ferguson ,Hilary Reno, MD, PhD, FIDSA , Theresa Carrington  and Nasir Abdalla  for gracing the event with their professionals insights.

To Maureen Wangard  and Jeffery L. Jackson, Ph.D., thank you so much for all you do for the African  Graduate students community at Saint Louis University . 

I am always excited seeing our community grow year on year.
