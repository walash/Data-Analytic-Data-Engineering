📍System design for data engineers is like architecture to builders!

🔘One of the key goals of system design for data engineers is to create systems that are both scalable and efficient. 

🔰That depicts that the system can handle increasing amounts of data without suffering from performance degradation or downtime.

🔰Also it defines that the system is designed to make the most efficient use of available resources, such as storage and processing power. 

🔰Another important aspect of system design for data engineers is ensuring that the system is secure and reliable. 

🔰This means that the system is designed to protect data from unauthorized access, and to ensure that the system remains operational even in the face of hardware or software failures. 

👩‍💻As per my experience, system design is a critical component of data engineering, and we as data engineers need to have a solid understanding of system design principles and practices to create effective data infrastructures that meet the needs of their organization. 

➡️Scalability, fault tolerance, and efficient data processing and storage are key considerations in data engineering system design.
