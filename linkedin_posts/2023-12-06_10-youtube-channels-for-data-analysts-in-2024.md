## Content

10 YouTube channels for Data Analysts In 2024:

1. Bas Dohmen (How to Power BI)

BI developer, Founder and Lead Trainer – datatraining.io

Topics: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, dashboard design

▶️ Start here: https://lnkd.in/eGwXeRHD

2. Alex Freberg (Alex The Analyst)

Analytics Manager, Founder Alex Analytics LLC

Topics: SQL, Python, Excel, Tableau, Power BI and interviewing tips

▶️ Start here: https://lnkd.in/e-TqcNRR

3. Chandeep Chhabra (Goodly)

Power BI Trainer and Consultant

Topics: Power BI, Excel, DAX and Power Query

▶️ Start here: https://lnkd.in/eBuEvMxx

4. Ruth Pozuelo Martinez (Curbal)

Owner of Curbal AB

Topics: Power BI, DAX

▶️ Start here: www.youtube.com/@CurbalEN

5. Patrick LeBlanc and Adam Saxton 
(Guy In A Cube)

Principal Program Managers at Microsoft

Topics: Power BI & Azure Synapse Analytics

▶️ Start here: https://lnkd.in/e37iHRMj

6. Joshua Starmer PhD (Statquest)

Founder and CEO at StatQuest and Lead AI Educator Lightning AI 

Topics: Explaining complex statistical concepts in simple terms.

▶️ Start here: www.youtube.com/@statquest

7. Lorenzo Rosa (Lore So What)

Data Analytics Lead @FAANG 

Topics: SQL, Python, Excel, Tableau and FAANG interview tips

▶️ Start here: https://lnkd.in/e2vWnR5c

8. Mike Girvin (ExcelIsFun)

Business Analyst & Technology Instructor

Topics: Power BI & Excel, DAX, Power Query, Power Pivot, M

▶️ Start here: https://lnkd.in/eQRN54Yw

9. Rick de Groot (BI Gorilla)

Microsoft® MVP, Power BI Consultant

Topics: Power BI, DAX, Power Query, M

▶️ Start here: www.youtube.com/@BIGorilla

10. Reid Havens (Havens Consulting)

Founder Havens Consulting, Microsoft MVP, BI Evangelist

Topics:  Power BI, PowerApps, and Microsoft Flow

▶️ Start here: https://lnkd.in/e62au2Rf
