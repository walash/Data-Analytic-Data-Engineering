## Content

These are all the resources that i used for my Software Engineering interview at Google.

📄 Resume Template: https://lnkd.in/gxZVBqqR
 - Clean, presentable one page resume format. Easy to format. 
 - Need to code in latex to edit the resume, but easy to do with AI search
 - There are other awesome one-column resume too, and they're just a google search away.

💭 Coding problems: Past 3 months google tagged problems
 - I wanted to focus on solving only the problems that were asked in Google
 interviews recently.
 - Speed matters during coding interviews. To get faster at solving problems, 
 i needed a strong grip on standard data structures and algorithms. To 
 reach that level, i solved standard problems few times. This helped me focus 
 on complex parts of problem during my interviews and not have to think 
 about basic concepts.
 - I would give a problem 10 mins, if i cannot do it in 10 mins, i would look up 
 the solution, understand different approaches and why they worked.

✍ Writing clean code: Cracking the coding interview
 - Writing code to solve a problem, isn't sufficient. Code needs to be simple, 
 clean, easy to understand and broken down into small functional pieces.
 - Went through few solutions in the book to learn how to code better.

🧓 Behavioral/Leadership: 
 - Wrote down my situations for standard leadership questions around 
 ambiguity, leadership, learning, team-work, bias for action. You can access 
 the list here - https://shorturl.at/MFdMp
 - Understanding google's culture was equally imp too. Watched video to 
 learn more about their culture and adapt my situations accordingly - 
 https://shorturl.at/ePEmW

🚀 How i gained confidence before my interviews: By solving recent google 
 interview problems in discussion section
 - I went through many google tagged posts in discussion section to 
 understand the kind of problems being asked recently. Being able to solve 
 most of those problems, gave me confidence for actual interviews.

💻 Mock Interviews: Topmate
 - Mock interviewed 3 times before onsite. Received very helpful feedback 
 around communication, coding faster, handling edge cases.

🧑 Guidance during preparation: Found mentor on LinkedIn
 - This was the game-changer in my journey. Found my mentor on linkedIn 
 who helped me build confidence, pushed me to apply for roles, gave me 
 invaluable insights on preparation and mock interviewed me.

Hopefully this is helpful for anyone planning to interview! 🎉
