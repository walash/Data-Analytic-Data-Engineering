## Content

There's no spiritual substitute for skills.

You can't achieve anything great with common sense and barbaric spiritual principles. King David was chosen and loved by God because of the skillfulness of his hands (skills) and integrity of his heart (values + skills + Attitude). He possessed great skills, knowledge, and capabilities. 

You can't pray to drive a car without driving skills, or else, you will get yourself killed.

An extraordinary Bishop of Faith is talking here.

Listen to him!

I won't employ a Christian without 21st century skills, attitude, and ability.
