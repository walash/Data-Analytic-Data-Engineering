Here are 10 prompts to create a standout resume:

1. Write a Strong Summary:
 "Create a compelling summary that highlights my skills and achievements for a [job position] at [company]."

2. Highlight Experience:
 "Write engaging descriptions of my work experience tailored to a [job position] at [company]."

3. Make it ATS-Friendly:
 "Optimize my resume with job-specific keywords to pass ATS for a [job position] at [company]."

4. Integrate into Cover Letter:
 "Summarize my resume for a cover letter that showcases my fit for a [job position] at [company]."

5. References Section:
 "List professional references relevant to a [job position] at [company]."

6. Language Skills:
 "Show how my multilingual abilities add value to [company] in the [job position]."

7. Technical Skills:
 "Highlight my technical expertise, certifications, and tools for a [job position] at [company]."

8. Soft Skills:
 "Demonstrate my soft skills with examples that align with a [job position] at [company]."

9. Create a Flexible Template:
 "Design a customizable resume template for different job applications."

10. Feedback & Edits:
 "Review and improve my resume for clarity, layout, and impact for a [job position] at [company]."
