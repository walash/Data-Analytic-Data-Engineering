What if the biggest data problem isn't the data itself — but how we approach it?

After analyzing data practices across industries, here's what research reveals:

Many companies struggle with these common challenges — and each has a clear path forward:

1️⃣ Collecting Data Without Clear Goals
55% of enterprise data sits unused ("dark data"), costing $1.7-3.3M/year in storage alone. The fix? Define KPIs first, then collect only what drives decisions. Orgs that reduce dark data below 30% see 1.8x faster decision cycles.

2️⃣ Building Dashboards Nobody Uses
40% of users say dashboards don't support decision-making. The opportunity: design user-centered dashboards with actionable insights, not just pretty charts. Target 65%+ utilization with role-specific views.

3️⃣ Underinvesting in Data Quality
Only 3% of companies meet basic quality standards, costing an average $15M/year per enterprise (Gartner). Apply the 1-10-100 rule — $1 to prevent errors at entry, $10 to correct after propagation, $100 to fix at the decision stage.

4️⃣ Working in Data Silos
82% of organizations cite data silos as their #1 barrier. Breaking them down with unified data catalogs improves operational efficiency by 20-25%.

The good news? Companies investing in proper data strategy see 348% ROI over 3 years (Forrester). That's the power of doing data right.
