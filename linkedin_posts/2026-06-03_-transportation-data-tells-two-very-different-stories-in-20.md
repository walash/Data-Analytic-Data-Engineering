🚛 Transportation Data Tells Two Very Different Stories in 2025

The global transportation sector is at a fascinating inflection point — and the data reveals a striking divergence between regions.

📊 Here's what the numbers say:

🇺🇸 United States: Freight is in a capacity-led recovery, not a demand surge. Trucking revenues dropped from $1T to $906B in 2024, yet dry van rates are rising ~8% YoY in 2026 — because carriers are exiting the market, not because volumes are booming. Meanwhile, EV adoption has stalled at just 7.5% of new vehicle sales for four consecutive quarters.

🌍 Africa: The continent is the world's fastest-growing aviation market. Airline RPK growth hit 13.2% YoY in 2024 — more than 2.5× the US rate — and international seat capacity is up 18.6% into 2026. Yet Africa carries only 250M air passengers vs. the US's 876M, despite representing 18% of the global population. The upside potential is enormous.

🌐 Global: Container port throughput rebounded to 920M TEUs in 2024 (+6.9%), and air cargo is forecast to reach 71.6M tonnes in 2026. But the logistics cost gap remains stark: Africa spends ~14.6% of GDP on logistics vs. 8.7% in the US — a structural drag that digital freight platforms are beginning to address.

The data analyst in me sees this as a sector ripe for disruption — from EV fleet transitions to AI-powered logistics optimization and African infrastructure investment.
