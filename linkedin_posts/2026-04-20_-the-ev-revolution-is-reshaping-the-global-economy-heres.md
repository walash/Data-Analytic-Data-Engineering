🚗 The EV Revolution Is Reshaping the Global Economy — Here's What the Data Shows

Global EV sales surged from 3.1M units in 2020 to 20.7M in 2025, now commanding 25.5% of new car sales worldwide. By 2030, EVs are projected to capture 62% of the market — effectively ending the ICE era.

📊 REGIONAL BREAKDOWN (2025-2026):
• China leads at 51% EV adoption, with BYD surpassing Tesla globally
• Europe surged 27% YoY in Q1 2026, hitting 33% market share
 The US lags at ~10%, but affordable models are driving recovery
• Africa remains at 1-2%, presenting massive untapped potential

 ECONOMIC IMPACT — The Numbers Are Staggering:
EVs displaced 1.7M barrels of oil/day in 2025, projected to reach 5M by 2030. The EV transition is creating 2.5M+ new jobs globally in manufacturing, batteries, and charging infrastructure — while displacing ~1M in ICE and oil sectors. Net positive: +1.5M jobs.

The global charging infrastructure market hit $39B in 2025, with $5B allocated under the US NEVI program alone. Battery costs dropped from $137/kWh in 2020 to ~$80/kWh in 2026, making EVs $8,000 cheaper to own over 5 years.

🌍 WHAT THIS MEANS FOR BUSINESS:
The $6.5 trillion EV market by 2030 demands data-driven strategy. Companies investing in electrification, supply chain analytics, and energy transition are positioned to lead. For data professionals, this sector offers some of the highest-paying roles in analytics, AI, and operations.

The transition isn't coming — it's here. The question is: who's ready to lead it?
