This resume got me #dataengineering interviews at Microsoft, Amazon, Deloitte, BCG, GS, CoinDCX and more!

Recruiters overlooked my Data Analyst experience, so I had to reshape my resume to break into Data Engineering. I focused on 3 key things:

✅ Optimized for scale: Highlighted projects where I built pipelines handling TBs of data, reduced processing time, or improved system efficiency.
✅ Tech stack with depth: Focused on Spark, Kafka, Airflow, and cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) with concrete examples of how I used them to solve problems.
✅ Business impact: Showed how my work led to faster insights, cost savings, or automation, not just that I built pipelines.

I spent a lot of time on my resume, got it reviewed by multiple people, and kept revising it until I received positive feedback.
